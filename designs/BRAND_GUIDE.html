The visual identity, UI language, and creative direction for the creator-brand marketplace

1. Logo

The PulseConnect logo is the primary brand mark. It should always be displayed prominently and with clear space around it.

Primary Logo (Dark Background)

Light Variant (Light Background)

Logo Usage: Always use the dark background variant on dark surfaces and the light variant on light surfaces. Minimum clear space = 1x the height of the "P" in PulseConnect. Minimum size: 32px height for digital, 0.5" for print.

10. Visual Style Summary

Dark-First Design

Deep space backgrounds (#0a0a0f) with elevated surfaces. Light mode is secondary and optional.

Gradient Accents

Violet → Cyan gradients used for primary CTAs, hero sections, and important UI elements.

Subtle Borders

Low-opacity white borders (rgba(255,255,255,0.06-0.1)) define surfaces without being harsh.

Generous Border Radius

12px for cards, 8px for buttons/inputs, 16px for sections. Soft, approachable, modern.

Monochromatic Data

Charts, stats, and data displays use mono cyan/purple tones rather than full rainbow.

Glow Effects

CTAs and active elements have subtle purple glow shadows for depth and emphasis.
